# Why Personal Colour Analysis Became Popular

<div style="background:#FAF1F3;border-left:5px solid #C97C8B;padding:24px;border-radius:12px;">

Personal Colour Analysis became a major beauty trend in Korea because people started focusing on **personalised beauty choices instead of following one universal standard**.

Instead of choosing colours simply because they are trending, this approach helps people understand which shades naturally complement their own features.

The trend became especially popular through Korean beauty creators, celebrities, and styling consultations where professionals analyse:

- Skin undertone
- Natural hair colour
- Eye colour
- Contrast level between features
- Best clothing shades
- Most flattering makeup tones

</div>

---

# What Is Personal Colour Analysis?

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #EBCFD6;border-radius:12px;padding:24px;">

Personal Colour Analysis is a method of identifying colours that harmonise with your natural appearance.

The concept is based on the idea that certain colours can:

- Make your complexion appear brighter
- Reduce the appearance of dullness
- Enhance facial features
- Create a more balanced overall look

Instead of changing your appearance, personal colour analysis helps you work with what you already have.

## The Main Factors Analysed

| Feature | What It Determines |
|----------|-------------------|
| Skin Undertone | Warm, cool, or neutral colour family |
| Hair Colour | Level of contrast and harmony |
| Eye Colour | Natural colour depth |
| Contrast Level | Bright vs soft colour suitability |

</div>

---

## How Korean Colour Analysis Works

<div style="background:#FDF6F8;border-left:5px solid #8B5E6C;padding:24px;border-radius:12px;">

In Korean colour analysis consultations, professionals usually place different coloured fabrics near the face and observe how each shade affects the person's appearance.

They analyse:

- Whether the skin appears brighter or duller
- Whether shadows become softer or stronger
- Which colours make facial features stand out naturally

The goal is not to restrict your choices but to help you understand which colours make you feel most confident.

</div>

# Understanding Skin Undertones

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #EBCFD6;border-radius:12px;padding:24px;">

Before choosing your ideal colour palette, the first step is understanding your **skin undertone**.

Your undertone is the subtle colour beneath your skin's surface. Unlike your skin tone, which can change with tanning or seasons, your undertone usually remains consistent.

There are three main undertone categories:

| Undertone | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Warm | Golden, peach, or yellow undertones |
| Cool | Pink, red, or blue undertones |
| Neutral | A balanced mix of warm and cool tones |

</div>

---

# Seasonal Colour Analysis Explained

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #EBCFD6;border-radius:12px;padding:24px;">

Korean personal colour analysis commonly uses the **four-season colour system**.

Each season represents a combination of:

- Temperature (warm or cool)
- Brightness
- Depth
- Softness

Your seasonal palette helps identify the colours that create the most harmony with your natural features.

# Common Personal Colour Analysis Mistakes

<div style="background:#FDF6F8;border-left:5px solid #C97C8B;padding:24px;border-radius:12px;">

Personal colour analysis is about enhancing your natural features, not restricting your choices.

Avoid these common mistakes while discovering your palette.

</div>

---

## 1. Choosing Colours Only Because They Are Trending

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #EBCFD6;border-radius:12px;padding:24px;">

Fashion trends change constantly, but your natural colouring remains the same.

A colour that looks amazing on someone else may not create the same effect on you.

### Better Approach:

Use trends as inspiration, then adapt them according to your personal colour palette.

</div>

---

## 2. Confusing Skin Tone With Undertone

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #EBCFD6;border-radius:12px;padding:24px;">

Your skin tone refers to how light or deep your complexion appears.

Your undertone refers to the subtle warm, cool, or neutral tones underneath.

Two people with the same skin tone can have completely different undertones.

</div>

---

## 3. Avoiding Colours Completely

<div style="background:#FFFFFF;border:1px solid #EBCFD6;border-radius:12px;padding:24px;">

Personal colour analysis should not limit your creativity.

If a colour you love is outside your recommended palette:

Try using it through:

- Accessories
- Shoes
- Bags
- Layering pieces
- Small makeup details

</div>

# Frequently Asked Questions

---

### What is personal colour analysis?

Personal colour analysis is a method of identifying colours that complement your natural features, including your skin undertone, hair colour, and eye colour.

---

### Is personal colour analysis only popular in Korea?

Although it became widely popular through Korean beauty and fashion culture, colour analysis is now used globally for styling, makeup, and wardrobe planning.

---

### Can my personal colour season change?

Your natural undertone usually stays the same, but factors like hair colour, tanning, and styling choices can influence how certain colours appear.

---

### How do I know if I have warm or cool undertones?

You can look at jewellery preference, vein colour, and how different clothing shades affect your complexion. Professional analysis provides a more detailed assessment.

---

### Can I wear colours outside my seasonal palette?

Yes. Personal colour analysis is a guide, not a rulebook. You can wear any colour by adjusting shades, styling, or placement.

---

### Does personal colour analysis help with makeup?

Yes. It helps you select lipstick, blush, eyeshadow, and foundation shades that create better harmony with your natural colouring.
